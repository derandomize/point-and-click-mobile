# NPC Portraits — `art/npc/<id>.png`

The engine composites characters over the background in the lower band of the
frame (`y` 55–90%) and picks the horizontal position itself (see `README.md`),
so a portrait needs **no in-image positioning** — just a clean full-body figure
on a transparent background.

## Shared portrait requirements

- Transparent background (alpha), no scene, no floor shadow.
- Full body, side / three-quarter view, facing toward the center of the frame.
- Same palette and linework as the backgrounds.
- Vertical framing, figure filling most of the height.
- No text, no UI, no frame.

Each prompt below is complete — replace nothing, just append nothing else
needed. Use the shared negative prompt at the bottom for every portrait.
