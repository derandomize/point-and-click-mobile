# Old Courier — `art/npc/courier.png`

Portrait is composited by the engine over the background in the lower band
(`y` 55–90%); the horizontal position is chosen at runtime, so no in-image
positioning is needed — just a clean full-body figure on a transparent
background, side / three-quarter view, facing toward the center of the frame.

## Prompt

> Full-body side-view character portrait for a hand-painted 2D point-and-click adventure, transparent background, no scene, no floor shadow, standing idle pose, vertical framing with the figure filling most of the height. A grey-haired, weathered old courier: worn satchel slung across the body, travel cloak, leaning on a walking cane. Soft warm lantern palette, visible imperfect ink linework, painterly shading, one burgundy accent. Cozy melancholic underground town inhabitant. No text, no UI, no frame.

> 3d render, photorealistic, anime, cel shading, sharp digital lines, neon, sci-fi, blood, gore, modern technology, text, watermark, logo, background, scenery.
